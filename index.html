How it works
  • You start with the same cash either way: the deposit plus buying costs. A buyer spends it on the house; a renter invests it.
  • Each month, both spend the same. A buyer pays the mortgage plus running costs, and a renter pays rent. Whoever's month is cheaper invests the difference.
  • Selling at any point: buyers get the home's value minus the mortgage left and selling costs, plus their investments. Renters have their investments.
  • Not included: tax on investment gains (assumes an ISA), inflation (figures are in future pounds), and moving more than once.
